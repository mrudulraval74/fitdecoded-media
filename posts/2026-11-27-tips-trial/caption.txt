🍽️ Which Indian foods actually give you protein? Save this list 👇

🧀 Paneer, 100 g → ~18g protein
🫛 Soya chunks, 30 g dry → ~15g
🍗 Chicken breast, 100 g cooked → ~31g
🐟 Fish, 100 g cooked → ~22g
🥚 2 boiled eggs → ~12g
🥣 Dal, dahi, chana, milk → ~7-9g each, and they add up!

✅ Pick 1 protein food in every meal
✅ Most people do well with ~20-30g per main meal
✅ Veg or non-veg, both work when you plan it
