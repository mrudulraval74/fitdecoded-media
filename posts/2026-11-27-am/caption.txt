🍬 Sugar-free mithai = guilt-free mithai? Let's decode it!

MYTH or FACT? 👉 Watch till the end.

❌ MYTH: "sugar-free" only means no added sugar, not no calories
🧈 Most mithai calories come from ghee, mawa and nuts
🥄 1 tsp ghee ≈ ~45 kcal, and mithai has a lot more than a teaspoon
🥜 Kaju and mawa are healthy foods, but very calorie-dense
😅 The "sugar-free" label makes it easy to eat 4 pieces instead of 1

✅ This wedding & festive season:
1️⃣ Pick one piece you really love and eat it slowly
2️⃣ Have fruit first when the sweet craving hits
3️⃣ Take an easy 10-minute walk after big meals
