💪 6 moves for a stronger chest: from home push-ups to dumbbells!

Start at move 1 if you're a beginner 👇 Watch each one, then save it for chest day.

1️⃣ Incline Push-up — chest + triceps. Hands on a bench or bed edge, body straight (12 reps)
2️⃣ Push-up — chest, shoulders, triceps. Elbows ~45°, chest close to the floor (8-12)
3️⃣ Wide Grip Push-up — chest + shoulders. Lower slowly, no sagging hips (8-10)
4️⃣ Dumbbell Press — chest + triceps. Shoulder blades back, press up (10-12)
5️⃣ Incline DB Press — upper chest + shoulders. Low incline, press up and slightly in (10-12)
6️⃣ Dumbbell Fly — chest. Soft elbows, go light (10-12)

🏋️ How to train it:
✅ 3 sets of each, rest ~60-90 sec
✅ Train chest 1-2 times a week
✅ Add a rep or a little weight when it feels easy

⚠️ Keep the weight light until your form is solid, and stop if anything hurts.
