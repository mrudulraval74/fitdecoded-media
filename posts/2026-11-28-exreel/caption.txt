🍑 Flat glutes? Build a strong butt at home with just a band + 2 dumbbells!

Beginner? Start at move 1 👇

1️⃣ Bodyweight Squat — glutes + quads. Chest up, knees follow toes (12-15)
2️⃣ Glute Bridge — glutes + hamstrings. Squeeze hard at the top (15)
3️⃣ Band Hip Abduction — side glutes. Stand tall, leg out slowly (12-15 each)
4️⃣ Band Glute Kickback — glutes. Core tight, don't arch your back (12-15 each)
5️⃣ Goblet Squat — glutes + quads. Dumbbell at chest, push through heels (10-12)
6️⃣ Dumbbell Lunge — glutes, quads, hamstrings. Big step, torso upright (8-10 each)

🏋️ How to train it:
✅ 3 sets of each, rest ~60-90 sec
✅ Train glutes 2 times a week
✅ Add a rep or a little weight when it feels easy

⚠️ Move slow and controlled, and stop if anything hurts.
