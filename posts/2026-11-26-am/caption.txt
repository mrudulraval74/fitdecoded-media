🥨 Namkeen with chai is a "light" snack? Let's decode it!

MYTH or FACT? 👉 Watch till the end.

❌ MYTH: namkeen and biscuits feel light, but they are calorie-dense
🔥 1 small katori (~30 g) of bhujia ≈ ~170 kcal
🍪 1 small biscuit ≈ ~25 kcal, and nobody stops at one
🧂 Fried + salty = easy to keep munching without feeling full
🫘 Roasted chana gives protein + fibre, so you stay full longer
🍿 Roasted makhana gives you a big bowl for far fewer calories

✅ Start today:
1️⃣ Put a small portion in a katori, never eat from the packet
2️⃣ Swap one namkeen snack a day for roasted chana or makhana
3️⃣ Pair your chai with fruit + a few peanuts
