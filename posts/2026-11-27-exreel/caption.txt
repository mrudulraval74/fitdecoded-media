💪 Flat shoulders? Build round delts with just 2 dumbbells + a band!

Beginner? Start at move 1 👇

1️⃣ Dumbbell Lateral Raise — side delts. Light weights, lift to shoulder height (12-15)
2️⃣ Dumbbell Front Raise — front delts. No swinging, lower slowly (10-12)
3️⃣ Alternate Shoulder Press — delts + triceps. Brace core, one arm at a time (8-10 each)
4️⃣ Dumbbell Arnold Press — front + side delts. Rotate palms out as you press (8-10)
5️⃣ Bent-over Rear Raise — rear delts, upper back. Flat back, arms wide (12-15)
6️⃣ Band Rear Delt Row — rear delts. Pull elbows wide and high (12-15)

🏋️ How to train it:
✅ 3 sets of each, rest ~60-90 sec
✅ Train shoulders 1-2 times a week
✅ Add a rep when it feels easy

⚠️ Go light on raises, keep form strict, and stop if anything hurts.
