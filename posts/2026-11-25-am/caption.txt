☕ 4 cups of chai a day is harmless? Let's do the sugar math!

MYTH or FACT? 👉 Swipe to find out.

❌ MYTH: chai itself is fine, the sugar is what adds up
🥄 1 tsp sugar ≈ ~4 g ≈ ~16 kcal
☕ 2 tsp per cup x 4 cups = ~8 tsp (~32 g) of sugar a day
📅 That's roughly ~900 kcal a week from chai sugar alone
🍪 2 biscuits with every cup add even more
🫘 Roasted chana or a handful of nuts keep you fuller than biscuits

✅ Start today:
1️⃣ Cut your sugar by half a spoon each week (your taste adjusts)
2️⃣ Go from 4 big cups to 2-3 small ones
3️⃣ Swap the biscuits for roasted chana or peanuts
