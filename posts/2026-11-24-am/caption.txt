❌ MYTH: that jump is mostly water, not fat
💧 Your body stores ~3 g of water with every gram of carbs
🔥 Gaining ~1 kg of real fat needs roughly ~7700 extra kcal — far more than one meal
🧂 Salty namkeen, pickle and papad make you hold extra water
🍽️ Food still in your stomach weighs something too
🏋️ A hard workout or your monthly cycle can shift water for a few days

✅ Start today:
1️⃣ Eat normally the next day — no skipping meals
2️⃣ Weigh in the morning, after the bathroom, same time daily
3️⃣ Track your weekly average, not one number
