💪 Sit all day? Build a strong back at home: 6 moves with a band + dumbbells!

Beginner? Start at move 1 👇

1️⃣ Band Bent-over Row — upper back, lats. Stand on the band, pull elbows back (12-15)
2️⃣ Band Seated Row — mid back. Sit tall, squeeze shoulder blades (12-15)
3️⃣ One-arm Band Pulldown — lats. Anchor high, pull elbow to your side (10-12 each)
4️⃣ One-arm DB Row — lats + upper back. Pull the dumbbell to your hip (10-12 each)
5️⃣ Dumbbell Deadlift — lower back, glutes, hamstrings. Hips back, flat back (10-12)
6️⃣ Inverted Row — upper back. Sturdy chairs + strong bar only, body straight (6-10)

🏋️ How to train it:
✅ 3 sets of each, rest ~60-90 sec
✅ Train back 1-2 times a week
✅ Add a rep when it feels easy

⚠️ Go light until your form is solid, and stop if anything hurts.
